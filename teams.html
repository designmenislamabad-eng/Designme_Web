Board of Directors

JA

Mr. Javaid Aslam

Chairman of the Board / Independent Director

Designation:

  • Chairman, Punjab Libraries Foundation
  • Member, Board of Management, Lahore General Hospital / PGMI

Profile:

Mr. Javaid Aslam (Hilal-e-Imtiaz) is a retired civil servant with over 40 years of distinguished service in senior government positions, primarily in Punjab. He also served as Secretary to the Prime Minister of Pakistan and President of the ECO Trade & Development Bank in Turkey.

He held key leadership roles in Punjab, including Chief Secretary, Chairman Planning & Development, Additional Chief Secretary, and Secretary of Excise & Taxation, Education, Transport and Labour. He also served as Commercial Counsellor in New York.

He holds master's degrees in Administrative Sciences from the University of York and English Literature from Punjab University, along with executive programs at Harvard, UNESCO and ILO.

MA

Mr. Muhammad Ali

Independent Director

Designation:

  • Managing Director, Sapphire Wind Projects
  • Managing Director, Sapphire Chemicals Private Limited
  • Managing Director, Sapphire new business initiatives

Profile:

Mr. Muhammad Ali has over 25 years of leadership experience in the energy and petrochemical sectors. He has held CEO and senior management roles at Engro Corporation and JS Group, and currently leads Sapphire Group's non-textile businesses, including power, chemicals and new initiatives.

He holds a B.E. in Electrical Engineering from UET Lahore and completed the Advanced Management Program at INSEAD, France.
